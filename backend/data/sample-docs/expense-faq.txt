Guidely Expenses FAQ

What expenses can I claim?
Reasonable business expenses, including approved travel, customer meetings, and work-related supplies, can be claimed when supported by an itemized receipt.

When should I submit an expense claim?
Submit claims through the Finance portal within 30 days of the purchase date. Claims submitted after 30 days need Finance approval and may be delayed.

What is the meal limit during business travel?
The meal allowance is up to 2,500 KES per day for domestic business travel. Alcohol is not reimbursable unless it is part of a pre-approved customer event.

How are reimbursements paid?
Approved claims submitted by the 20th of the month are normally paid with the following month's payroll. Finance may request clarification or additional receipts before approving a claim.
