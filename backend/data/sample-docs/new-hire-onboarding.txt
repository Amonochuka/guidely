Guidely New Hire Onboarding Guide

First week
During your first week, complete the security-awareness training, set up multi-factor authentication, and meet your manager to agree on your first 30-day goals. HR will provide access to the employee portal and benefits information.

Equipment
New employees receive a company laptop and standard accessories. Submit additional equipment requests through the IT service desk. Return company equipment to IT when your employment ends.

Learning and development
Each full-time employee has an annual learning budget of 30,000 KES. The budget may be used for approved courses, books, professional memberships, or conferences. Get manager approval before making a purchase and submit receipts within 30 days for reimbursement.

Probation
The standard probation period is six months. Your manager will hold check-ins at approximately 30, 60, and 90 days to discuss progress and support.
