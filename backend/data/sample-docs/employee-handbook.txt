Guidely Employee Handbook

Working hours
Standard working hours are Monday to Friday, 8:30 AM to 5:00 PM East Africa Time. Employees should inform their manager before changing their regular schedule.

Annual leave
Full-time employees receive 21 paid annual-leave days each calendar year. Leave requests should be submitted in the HR portal at least five working days before the first day of leave. Managers approve requests based on team coverage.

Sick leave
Employees may take up to 10 paid sick-leave days each year. Notify your manager as soon as possible on the first day of absence. A medical note may be required for absences of three consecutive working days or more.

Remote work
Employees may work remotely for up to two days each week with their manager's approval. They must be available during agreed working hours and follow the company's information-security requirements.
